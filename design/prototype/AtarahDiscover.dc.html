How to price commissioned illustration in Kenya

Kiptoo Langat · 6 min read · 9 Oct 2026

Most early-career illustrators under-price because they quote by the hour. Clients don't buy hours — they buy usage, reach and reliability.

Start with a day rate that covers your costs and the time you can't bill, then add a licence fee based on where the work will appear and for how long.

Rule of thumb: an editorial spot and a national billboard are not the same job, even if they take the same afternoon.
